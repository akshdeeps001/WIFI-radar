03 // $ man wifi-sensing — the brief version

how it's actually done

six mechanisms, one screen each — the real pipeline academic + consumer systems run.

rf --explain capture
every OFDM subcarrier arrives bent by the room:
amplitude + phase per subcarrier = CSI / CFR.
64 subcarriers × ~100 pkt/s ≈ a 6,400-value
"camera" with no lens, sampled 100×/s.
802.11bf standardizes asking devices for exactly this.
rf --explain detect
keep a slow average of CSI = baseline B (empty room).
motion(t) = | CSI(t) − B | > θ → 1 bit: someone moved
radar MTI reborn — exactly what the ghost view
+ echo line in the sim compute.
rf --explain speed
walking shifts the echo's phase; differentiate it:
f_d = 2·v/λ   (λ = 12.5 cm @ 2.4 GHz)
≈ 16 Hz per m/s → walk 1.5 m/s ≈ 24 Hz, run ≈ 55 Hz.
the RX panel's DOPPLER row is this formula, live.
rf --explain gait
torso reflects a steady band; feet/arms swing around it
as sinusoids — the micro-Doppler signature.
limp → asymmetric; sneak → compressed to zero.
KIT "BFId" (ACM CCS): 197 people, 99.5% re-ID
from unencrypted beamforming feedback. no phone carried.
rf --explain locate
coarse: motion energy per room/zone — the thermal map.
fine: angle-of-arrival + time-of-flight across the
antenna array → ~1 m indoors. mesh nodes give
per-room zoning for free.
rf --explain limits
still body → only breathing survives (0.1–0.5 mm chest
motion — measurable!). metal reflects → room shielded.
concrete −15…−30 dB. −20 dB per 10 m:
falloff is the honest defense your floor plan gives you.
