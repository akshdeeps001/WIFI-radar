# 📡 WIFI RADAR

### Your Wi-Fi is a radar. His laptop is the screen.

A black-terminal interactive simulation of Wi-Fi sensing through walls.

Drive a character through a 3D six-room house — and watch a neighbor leeching your signal rebuild your every move from radio echoes alone: which room you're in, how fast you're moving, whether the moving thing is you or the cat.

**No camera. No microphone. Nothing installed on your devices. Just the same radio waves that carry your internet.**
